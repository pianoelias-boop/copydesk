# Email Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own email editing skill.

- Subject line: under ~50 characters, specific, curiosity or value — never bait that the body doesn't pay off.
- Preview text should extend the subject line, not repeat it.
- The first line earns the second. No throat-clearing ("Hope this finds you well", "We're excited to announce").
- Write to one reader, not a list. "You" over "our customers".
- Scannability: short paragraphs (1–3 lines), meaningful line breaks, bold only for the one thing that must not be missed.
- One primary CTA, repeated at most once. Every additional ask costs clicks on the main one.
- The P.S. is prime real estate — if there is one, it should carry the offer or the deadline, not a shrug.
