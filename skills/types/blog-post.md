# Article Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own blog/article editing skill.

- The intro must establish the payoff within the first two paragraphs: what the reader will know or be able to do by the end.
- Cut warm-up paragraphs. Most drafts start one to three paragraphs before the actual beginning.
- Headers should be informative standing alone — a reader skimming only headers should get the argument.
- Each section delivers on its header within the first sentence or two.
- Examples beat exposition: where the draft explains an idea abstractly twice, replace one pass with a concrete example.
- Kill "as mentioned above/below" cross-references; restructure so they're unnecessary.
- End on the strongest implication or next step, not a recap.
