# Landing Page Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own landing-page editing skill.

- The headline must pass the 5-second test: a stranger should know what's offered and why it matters. Clarity beats cleverness — clever-but-vague headlines get rewritten clear.
- Subhead does the explaining the headline can't: who it's for, what it does, the key differentiator.
- Match copy hierarchy to skimmers: section headers should tell the whole story read alone.
- Above-the-fold copy earns the scroll; don't bury the value proposition in paragraph three.
- Social proof should be specific (name, role, metric) — tighten vague testimonials' framing.
- Objection handling: every major hesitation (price, effort, risk) gets addressed near the CTA.
- The CTA should restate value, not process. "Start free — no card required" beats "Sign up".
