# Case Study Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own case study editing skill.

- Structure check: problem → stakes → solution → results. If results appear before the problem is felt, restructure.
- The customer is the hero; the product is the tool. Edit sentences where the vendor stars in the customer's story.
- Quantify the before-state as well as the after-state — improvement is meaningless without a baseline.
- Pull the single best metric into the opening; don't make readers dig for the payoff.
- Customer quotes should carry emotion and judgment; narrative text carries facts and numbers.
- Make the "solution" section concrete about what changed in the customer's actual workflow, not a feature list.
- Close with applicability: help the reader see themselves in the story.
