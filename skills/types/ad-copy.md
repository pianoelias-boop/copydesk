# Ad Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own ad copy editing skill.

- Every word fights for its place — ads have hard character limits, so density of meaning is the top criterion.
- Lead with the benefit or the hook, never the brand name (unless the brand is the hook).
- Match the message to intent: search ads answer the query; social ads interrupt, so they must earn the interruption.
- Numbers and specifics outperform superlatives: "Setup in 4 minutes" beats "Incredibly fast setup".
- The headline and description should each work alone AND together — no headline that's meaningless without the description.
- CTA must be concrete and low-friction: what happens when they click, in their words.
- Cut any word doing brand-voice decoration rather than persuasion work.
