# Social Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own social media editing skill.

- The first line is the whole game — it must stop the scroll on its own, before any "see more" fold.
- One post, one idea. If the draft makes three points, the edit makes the strongest one and cuts the rest.
- Conversational register: contractions, direct address, sentence fragments allowed where natural.
- No hashtag stuffing; at most a couple, and only if they're how the audience actually finds content.
- Cut engagement-bait formulas ("Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Let that sink in") unless the author's voice genuinely uses them.
- Line breaks are punctuation on social — use white space to pace the read.
- The close should invite a specific response or action, not a generic "learn more".
