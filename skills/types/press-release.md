# Press Release Craft

> **PLACEHOLDER SKILL** — replace with your own press release editing skill.

- Inverted pyramid: the first paragraph must contain who, what, when, and why it matters. A journalist should be able to write a story from paragraph one alone.
- Headline in plain news register — no marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "industry-leading" get cut).
- Quotes should say something only a human could say: opinion, stakes, intention. Quotes that restate the facts of the announcement get rewritten or flagged.
- Numbers and proper nouns over adjectives. Every claim a journalist would have to verify should come with its source.
- Keep the company boilerplate to the boilerplate section.
- Third person throughout; no "we" outside quotes.
