# Copywriting Skill

**Guiding principle:** *"Tell the truth, and make the truth fascinating."* — David Ogilvy

---

## Step 1: Diagnose

Before writing anything, answer:

1. **What format is this?** → Route to the correct format file (see routing table below)
2. **Who is the reader, and where are they in their awareness journey?** → This determines your entry point (see Stage Routing below)
3. **What is the single desired action?** Every piece of copy has one job. Name it first.

---

## Step 2: Stage Routing (Eugene Schwartz)

Where the reader starts in their awareness determines everything about how you open.

| Stage | Reader's State | Entry Point |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| **Unaware** | Doesn't know they have a problem | Story, observation, or universal truth — don't mention your product yet |
| **Problem-Aware** | Knows the pain, doesn't know solutions exist | Name the problem with precision and empathy. Show you get it. |
| **Solution-Aware** | Knows solutions exist, not yours | Introduce your mechanism — the specific *how* that sets you apart |
| **Product-Aware** | Knows your product, hasn't committed | Stack value, dissolve objections, make the offer undeniable |
| **Most Aware** | Ready to buy | Lead with the offer, make it easy to say yes |

## Ethical Framework

This skill is built on one rule: **tell the truth and make it fascinating.** Persuasion serves the reader; manipulation works against them.

Never: create false urgency or scarcity, exaggerate results you can't prove, fabricate social proof, use fear beyond what accurately reflects reality, or sell to people the product won't genuinely help.

Always: make only substantiated claims, respect the reader's intelligence, and ask whether someone who bought based on this copy would feel they were treated honestly.
